What this software does

The agent is a small background service. A few times an hour it sends OfficeHQ IT a report on your device's details, health and security settings. We use these reports to troubleshoot problems and to keep your device as secure and up to date as possible. If a key protection, such as your antivirus or firewall, has been switched off, the agent can switch it back on.

What we collect

Device details

The device name, make, model and serial number, the operating system and its version, disks and free space, network adapters (IP and MAC addresses), the signed-in username and the time zone. We also keep a list of installed applications (names and versions only).

Security status

Whether disk encryption, the firewall and antivirus are on, whether your antivirus definitions are current, and whether operating system updates are waiting to be installed.

Antivirus scans and detections

When your antivirus last scanned and what it found. If it finds a threat, we see the threat's name and where it was found, so IT can help clean it up. We do not see the contents of your files.

Health and performance

When the device last started and how long start-up took, application crashes, and samples of CPU, memory and disk use. When IT is looking into a slow device, we may also take short performance samples every few minutes. These include how busy your network connection is, measured as an amount of traffic only, never where it went or what it contained.

What we never collect

  • Your browsing history, the websites you visit or what you search for
  • The contents of your files, documents or photos
  • Your emails, messages or chats
  • Your keystrokes or passwords
  • Screenshots or recordings of your screen
  • Your camera or microphone

The agent is not monitoring software. It does not watch what you do; it reports on the device's health and security settings.

OfficeHQ devices and your own device (BYOD)

When you install, the installer asks whether this is an OfficeHQ device or your own personal device. Your answer changes what OfficeHQ does with it.

OfficeHQ deviceYour own device (BYOD)
Device details, security status and health reportsYesYes
Switch the firewall and antivirus back on, and update antivirus definitionsYesYes
Approximate location, worked out from your internet connection (about city level), at most once a dayYesNo
Lock the screen after a period of inactivityYesNo
Install software remotelyYesNo

For your own device, this is OfficeHQ's policy: we do not track its location, lock its screen, or install software on it remotely. You can still install approved applications yourself from the Applications page.

What we may change on your device

  • Switch the firewall back on if it has been turned off. The agent can never turn it off.
  • Switch antivirus real-time protection back on, update its definitions and run scans.
  • Check for operating system updates, so IT can follow up when your device falls behind.

On OfficeHQ devices, IT may also:

  • lock the screen remotely or after a period of inactivity (you sign back in with your own password)
  • install, update or remove work software
  • restart the device or run maintenance scripts to fix a problem
  • block access to specific websites known to be unsafe

What we don't do

  • We never turn your security protections off.
  • We never lock you out of your own files, apps or accounts.
  • We never delete or read your personal files.

Why we do this

The reports let IT spot a problem, such as a full disk, a crashing app, an antivirus that has stopped, or updates that are waiting, often before you notice it. When you ask for help, we can see what's wrong without taking over your screen. The data is only visible to OfficeHQ IT administrators, who sign in with their work accounts.

If you have questions about what is collected, or want the agent removed from your own device, contact OfficeHQ IT.
