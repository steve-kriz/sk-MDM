OfficeHQ MDM Agent

This installs the OfficeHQ device management agent on this Mac.

During the install you will be asked to confirm the collection URL and whether this is an OfficeHQ device or your own (BYOC). When it finishes, your browser opens so you can sign in with your OfficeHQ account; the device enrolls itself once you have signed in.

If you skip the sign-in, open Sign in to OfficeHQ MDM in Applications › OfficeHQ at any time.
